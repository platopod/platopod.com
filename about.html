About

Made in Canberra.

Plato Pod is built by a small team at UNSW Canberra, the university's campus at the Australian Defence Force Academy.

The people

AL

Artem Lenskiy

Founder, principal architect and software

Started Plato Pod, designed its architecture and writes most of its software. Lecturer at UNSW Canberra and the Australian War College, teaching communication and information systems and surveillance technologies, and an Honorary Senior Lecturer at the University of Sydney.

PhD in computer and electrical engineering from the University of Ulsan, South Korea (2010), funded by the Agency for Defense Development. Earlier work includes the vision system for the XM1219 armed robotic vehicle, and an autonomous vehicle for a competition funded by Hyundai Motor and the Agency for Defense Development.

Rhianne Chng

Principal engineer

Electrical Team Lead of the UNSW Canberra Robotics and Autonomous Systems Team, which placed fourth overall at the 2026 Australian Rover Challenge. Winner of the 2020 UNSW Canberra Prize for Best Female Student in Mathematics. Works on circuit board design and robotics.

G

Gino

Country representative, the Philippines

Brings Plato Pod to partners in the Philippines.
