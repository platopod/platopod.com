The robots are the cheap part.

What makes an exercise worth running is everything the robots cannot see. All of it is evaluated against one world, every tick.

01
Engagement
Every shot resolved against weapon, line of sight and target state. Damage degrades mobility and the ability to return fire.
02
Rules of engagement
Weapons hold, tight or free, with civilian and friendly checks applied before a round is resolved.
03
Line of sight
Cover and elevation block observation — a fast ray model, or a lidar backend behind the same interface.
04
Electronic warfare
Emitters, jammed regions and communications dead zones. A unit can be cut off without being touched.
05
Sensing
Gas with stateful detector dynamics, lidar, sonar, thermal, rangefinder, IED detection, direction finding, GNSS, UAV camera, friend-or-foe.
06
Logistics
Fuel, ammunition and water, consumed and resupplied, with weather and visibility over the top.
07
Interoperability
Cursor on Target: positions, zones, sensor readings and engagement events bridge to ATAK.
