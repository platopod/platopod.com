Real robots. Virtual force. One arena.

Plato Pod puts small robots on a desk and builds the battlefield around them in software: sensors, an opposing force, gas, fire and rules of engagement. Students learn to program and to plan with it. Officers rehearse on it.

Get in touch → How it works

What it is

Three things, working as one.

  1. 1

    Pods on the desk

    Small robots, 70 mm across, driven over Bluetooth and tracked by one overhead camera that reads a printed marker on each.

  2. 2

    A world around them

    Virtual units, simulated sensors, hazards and an opposing force share the arena with the pods, so a few robots stand in for a larger force.

  3. 3

    One picture

    The exercise shows in a browser, and on ATAK phones and tablets, placed on real ground.
